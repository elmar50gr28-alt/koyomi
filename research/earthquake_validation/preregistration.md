# Preregistration

## Primary Question

Do high research-only mundane scores correspond to increased subsequent earthquake activity in predefined windows, compared with controls?

## Fixed Primary Evaluation

- Time windows: 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days
- Spatial windows: 100, 300, 500, 1000 km
- Primary magnitude threshold: M4.5+
- Primary metric: event count and total seismic moment
- Primary catalog: USGS ComCat

## Controls

- Same region and season
- High-score periods without major earthquakes
- Low-score periods
- Random periods
- Year-shifted pseudo-controls, only after confirming catalog activity

## Reporting Rule

All conditions are reported. The best-looking condition is not presented alone. Negative, null, false alarm, and missed-event results are retained.
